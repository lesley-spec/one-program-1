9 Nav Restructures
Each persona reshapes the L1/L2 nav around their daily job-to-be-done. Every card carries a designer note on what moved in the IA and why.
👩🏾
Aisha Thompson
🛒 Walmart
Senior Affiliate & Creator Partnerships Manager
"I know something's wrong before I even open the platform — I just can't find where the problem is fast enough."
Daily portfolio scan Check-in prep Creator campaign monitoring Spot at-risk partners early
148 Partners · 5+ Programs
V1
Portfolio Radar
Anomaly-first cross-program roll-up
What changed
  • Home replaced with a cross-program Program Pulse anomaly-triage inbox — no more per-program toggling
  • Partners segmented by health status (At Risk / Watch / Winners / Healthy) instead of by program
  • Performance defaults to cross-program compare; per-program is a filter, not a nav destination
  • Contracts & Rates demoted to a mid-tier item (monthly cadence, not daily)
Aisha's #1 pain is toggle friction and missing dips. Every L1 answers a daily-scan question. Segmenting by health status makes the "who needs my eyes" answer show up on the first click.
Proposed nav
  • 📡 Program Pulse NEW L1
    • Anomalies today
    • Attention queue
    • Cross-program snapshot
  • 🤝 Partners
    • At Risk 4
    • Watch
    • Winners
    • Healthy
    • All Partners
    • Discover / Recruit
  • 📊 Performance
    • Cross-program compare default
    • By partner · by product · by channel
    • Exports & scheduled
  • 🎬 Creator Campaigns
  • 📜 Contracts & Rates demoted
  • 💸 Finance
  • 🛡 Protect
  • ⚙ Settings
V2
Signals & Actions
Split "notice" from "act"
What changed
  • Home splits into two rails: Live Signals (anomalies, dips, spikes) and My Queue (check-ins, bonuses, outreach)
  • Partners flat: Roster / At Risk / Winners — no folder hierarchy
  • Insights folds Optimize + Competitive Insights into one section (reduces L1 count)
  • Automations promoted so Aisha can wire up the queue side without leaving her landing surface
Aisha's day is triage. Separating what she needs to notice from what she needs to act on reduces cognitive load — she doesn't have to re-scan cards she's already triaged this morning.
Proposed nav
  • 🏠 Home
    • Live Signals ← anomalies, dips, spikes
    • My Queue ← today's actions
  • 🤝 Partners
    • My Roster
    • At Risk 4
    • Winners
    • Recruit / Discover
  • 🔬 Insights merged
    • Performance
    • Attribution & funnels
    • Competitive benchmarks
    • Data Lab
  • 🎬 Campaigns
  • ⚡ Automations promoted
  • 📜 Contracts
  • 💸 Finance
  • 🛡 Protect
V3
Segments-First
Nav rooted in her portfolio slices
What changed
  • New L1 Segments is the primary noun — each category × program (grocery, electronics, fashion × affiliate/creator)
  • Every segment holds its own full stack inline: partners, performance, campaigns, contracts
  • Partners demoted; Discover still surfaces recruit tools but browsing shifts to Segments
  • Home = segment health grid — one row per segment, dots colored by anomaly severity
Aisha thinks in categories × programs, not in a flat partner list. Making Segments the L1 mirrors her mental model, so every nav interaction happens inside the slice she's already thinking about.
Proposed nav
  • 🏠 Home
    • Segment health grid
    • Attention queue
  • 🧩 Segments NEW L1
    • Grocery · Affiliate
    • Grocery · Creator
    • Electronics · Affiliate
    • Fashion · Creator
    • …per-segment stack: partners, perf, campaigns, contracts
  • 🤝 Partners demoted
    • All Partners (flat directory)
    • Discover / Recruit
  • 📊 Performance
  • 📜 Contracts
  • 💸 Finance
  • 🛡 Protect
